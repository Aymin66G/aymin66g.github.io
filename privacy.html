BarqPhone Backup - Privacy Policy

BarqPhone Backup is a private backup solution for the BarqPhone ERP system.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive through Rclone to upload, retrieve, and manage encrypted backup files.

Data Protection

Business backups are encrypted locally with Restic before uploading them to Google Drive.

Data Sharing

We do not sell business data or Google user data to third parties.

Credentials

Google authorization tokens are stored locally for the backup process and are not published on this website.

Access Revocation

The Google account owner can revoke application access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Data Deletion

The account owner can delete stored backups from Google Drive and remove locally stored authorization credentials.

Purpose

This application is intended for private business backup and recovery.
